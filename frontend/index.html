How it works

Three simple steps to your shared ride.

1

Post or search a ride

Drop a start and destination pin on the map, pick your time, seats and vehicle.

2

Match smartly

We only show rides starting and ending within 100 m of yours, within 15 minutes of your time.

3

Chat & go

Once the owner accepts, a private chat opens. Meet up, ride together and split the cost.
